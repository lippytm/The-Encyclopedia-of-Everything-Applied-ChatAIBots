## Overview

The Encyclopedia of Everything Applied is a living, evolving knowledge base that blends real-world software engineering, blockchain development, artificial intelligence, robotics, Life and Business Sciences, and speculative futurist concepts to teach people — and machines — how to continuously grow, adapt, build, test, correct, and steward better systems.

This encyclopedia is structured around the idea that **learning is infinite** and that the same principles governing biological evolution, social networks, business ecosystems, environmental systems, and imagined intergalactic civilizations can be applied to becoming a capable programmer, blockchain developer, creator, systems thinker, and responsible innovator.

See the `/docs` folder for deep-dive articles on each topic.

## Teaching People & Robots to Be Better Programmers

> *"The best teacher is a system that adapts to its student — whether that student is human or silicon."*

This is the instructional core of the encyclopedia. It covers methods, frameworks, and tools for accelerating programming mastery in both humans and robotic/AI systems.

### For Humans

- **Project-based learning** — build real things; learn by doing, testing, explaining, and correcting.
- **Peer code review culture** — structured feedback loops that make every pull request a teaching moment.
- **The Feynman Technique applied to code** — if you cannot explain your function clearly, you probably do not yet understand it fully.
- **Earn-while-you-Learn pathways** — open-source bounties, hackathon prizes, bug bounties, educational products, and freelance microtasks with transparent rules and no guaranteed-income claims.
- **Character-guided learning** — use fictional guides, witnesses, builders, and red-team critics to make difficult systems memorable.

### For Robots & AI Systems

- **Curriculum learning** — progressively harder programming, reasoning, and quality-control tasks.
- **Code generation and self-debugging** — models that write, test, critique, and revise code under human-defined permissions.
- **Robotic process automation plus smart contracts** — controlled interactions among workflows, blockchains, and business rules.
- **Feedback-driven improvement** — documented corrections, tests, human review, revocation, and retirement rather than unlimited autonomous modification.
- **Agent and Character Passports** — provenance, permissions, data limits, model identity, and audit history.

## AI Copilot Video Explainer / Tutorial / Sandbox Creator (ACVS)

> *"A video is not a static artifact. It is a living lesson — aware of who is watching, what they already know, where they are struggling, and what credential they will earn when they succeed."*

The **AI Copilot Video Explainer / Tutorial / Video Sandbox Creator (ACVS)** is the ACSS creative production system that merges Hermes routing and Fabric knowledge into three distinct video modes — Explainer, Tutorial, and Sandbox — forming the backbone of the Earn-while-you-Learn multimedia delivery pipeline.

Key concepts explored:

- **Three video modes** — Explainer (concept-first, animated, narrative), Tutorial (step-by-step build walkthroughs with terminal recordings), Sandbox (interactive build missions with GESN credential gates).
- **Hermes creative event bus** — 9 typed events govern the full pipeline: `CREATE_VIDEO_REQUEST` → `VIDEO_SCRIPT_READY` → `SCENE_PRODUCTION_COMPLETE` → `QUALITY_GATE_PASS` → `G13_APPROVED` → `VIDEO_PUBLISHED` → `LEARNER_MILESTONE` → `IMPROVEMENT_REQUIRED` → `SCRIPT_REVISION_COMPLETE`.
- **Fabric knowledge integration** — `FabricVideoNode` graph stores every video as a node linked to concept nodes, learner progress, quiz results, GESN drop-off data, and cross-references; ACVSScriptAgent queries Fabric before writing every script to prevent repetition and target weak spots.
- **`ACVSScriptAgent`** — lippytmai clone in Teach mode; queries Fabric for related nodes and learner weak spots, builds GPT-4o prompts, generates scene manifest JSON, dispatches Hermes `VideoScriptReady` event.
- **`SandboxSession`** — per-learner interactive mission: progressive challenges, hint system, build gate verification (artifact hash check), credential mint on pass.
- **ACVS Video Taxonomy** — 10 categories (`VID-CLL-L0`, `VID-CSEL-L0`, `VID-CBSLL-L0`, `VID-ACSS-L0`, `VID-OMARCHY`, `VID-SANDBOX`, `VID-COPILOT`, `VID-GESN`, and more) stored in Fabric and surfaced in GESN.
- **Continuous Improvement Cycle** — GESN analytics → Fabric weak spot detection → Hermes `IMPROVEMENT_REQUIRED` → ACVSScriptAgent targeted scene revision → G13 re-review (scenes only).
- **Sandbox Docker runtime** — `docker-compose.sandbox.yml` with `acvs-sandbox` web terminal container, `acvs-challenge-api` FastAPI service, and PostgreSQL for session persistence.
- **ACVS + HDVG relationship** — ACVS is the creative pipeline (scripts, modes, session logic, Hermes/Fabric integration); HDVG is the production pipeline (renders scene manifests into MP4/WebM/HLS). They are complementary, not competing.

## Creative Building Process

 is the unified methodology that governs how all lippytm.ai content is conceived, created, quality-reviewed, published, and continuously improved. It merges Hermes event routing, Fabric knowledge context, ACVS video production, and ADA deployment into a single repeatable loop:

**8-Stage Creative Loop:**
1. **INTENT** — Charles defines topic via P011-PLAN-001
2. **CONTEXT** — Fabric loads prior books, learner weak spots, cross-links
3. **CREATE** — lippytmai drafts ebook (11 chapters + build artifact)
4. **QUALITY REVIEW** — QualityReviewEngine runs G1–G12 automated gates

6. **PRODUCE** — ACVS generates Explainer, Tutorial, and Sandbox videos
7. **DEPLOY** — ADA activates book, credential minted on Base
8. **IMPROVE** — GESN analytics feed back into Fabric for continuous revision

- `lippytmai` — all ebook drafting, video scripts, teaching content
- `lippytm` — builds, CI/CD, ADA deployment
