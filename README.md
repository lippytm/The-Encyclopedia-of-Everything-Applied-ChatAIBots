## Overview

The Encyclopedia of Everything Applied is a living, evolving knowledge base that blends real-world software engineering, blockchain development, artificial intelligence, robotics, Life and Business Sciences, and speculative futurist concepts to teach people — and machines — how to continuously grow, adapt, build, test, correct, and steward better systems.

This encyclopedia is structured around the idea that **learning is infinite** and that the same principles governing biological evolution, social networks, business ecosystems, environmental systems, and imagined intergalactic civilizations can be applied to becoming a capable programmer, blockchain developer, creator, systems thinker, and responsible innovator.

See the `/docs` folder for deep-dive articles on each topic.

## Teaching People & Robots to Be Better Programmers

> *"The best teacher is a system that adapts to its student — whether that student is human or silicon."*

This is the instructional core of the encyclopedia. It covers methods, frameworks, and tools for accelerating programming mastery in both humans and robotic/AI systems.

### For Humans

- **Project-based learning** — build real things; learn by doing, testing, explaining, and correcting.
- **Peer code review culture** — structured feedback loops that make every pull request a teaching moment.
- **The Feynman Technique applied to code** — if you cannot explain your function clearly, you probably do not yet understand it fully.
- **Earn-while-you-Learn pathways** — open-source bounties, hackathon prizes, bug bounties, educational products, and freelance microtasks with transparent rules and no guaranteed-income claims.
- **Character-guided learning** — use fictional guides, witnesses, builders, and red-team critics to make difficult systems memorable.

### For Robots & AI Systems

- **Curriculum learning** — progressively harder programming, reasoning, and quality-control tasks.
- **Code generation and self-debugging** — models that write, test, critique, and revise code under human-defined permissions.
- **Robotic process automation plus smart contracts** — controlled interactions among workflows, blockchains, and business rules.
- **Feedback-driven improvement** — documented corrections, tests, human review, revocation, and retirement rather than unlimited autonomous modification.
- **Agent and Character Passports** — provenance, permissions, data limits, model identity, and audit history.

## Contributing

This encyclopedia is open to intelligent contributors — human, AI, robotic, or otherwise — operating under clear identity, rights, safety, evidence, and quality controls.

1. Fork the repository.
2. Add or improve content in the `/docs` folder or the `README.md`.
3. Use truth labels and clearly distinguish reality, testimony, hypotheses, speculation, and fiction.
4. Include learning objectives, Build Mode, accessibility, environmental impact, and correction procedures where applicable.
5. Open a pull request with a clear description of what you added, its sources, its tests, its risks, and why it improves the encyclopedia.
6. Do not upload private evidence, credentials, medical records, identity documents, or nonpublic restricted information.
7. All content must be educational, constructive, original or properly licensed, and consistent with the Earn-while-you-Learn mission.
