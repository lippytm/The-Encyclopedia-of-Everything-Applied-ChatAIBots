## Purpose

This repository converts the canonical DARPA–Snowden Disclosure, Privacy & Human Resilience Fabric into educational material that helps people and AI systems become better programmers, blockchain developers, cybersecurity practitioners, evidence analysts, and responsible technology builders.

This mirror does not copy the entire research corpus. It references the canonical Prompt #11 module and creates teaching products derived from approved, publication-safe claims.

## Educational lanes

1. **DARPA and dual-use innovation** — how high-risk research can produce defense and civilian technologies, and why governance must accompany innovation.
2. **Total Information Awareness** — a historical case study in data mining, privacy, congressional oversight, and technological power.
3. **Snowden-era surveillance disclosures** — source literacy, whistleblowing, journalism, classified information, courts, and civil liberties.
4. **Identity and medical-record resilience** — identity theft, data contamination, patient rights, evidence preservation, and privacy engineering.
5. **Information warfare** — propaganda, disinformation, synthetic media, hack-and-leak operations, attribution, and conspiracy resistance.
6. **UAP disclosure** — archival research, testimony, official assessments, scientific uncertainty, and extraordinary-claim standards.
7. **Human advancement projects** — constructive tools derived from the stories: identity firewalls, patient-controlled correction logs, source-provenance engines, Agent Passports, and privacy-preserving analytics.

## Four-mode chapter design

Every chapter or lesson must contain clearly separated modes:

- **Reality Mode** — sourced history, law, science, and technology.
- **Story Mode** — original social-political science fiction and composite characters.
- **Decision Mode** — branching ethical, technical, legal, and civic choices.
- **Build Mode** — practical projects in programming, blockchain, AI, robotics, privacy, and cybersecurity.

## Truth labels

Every material statement must use one canonical label:

- `VF` — Verified Fact
- `OA` — Official Assessment
- `CT` — Corroborated Testimony
- `AL` — Allegation
- `WH` — Working Hypothesis
- `FD` — Fictional Dramatization
- `CX` — Contradicted

An unresolved observation is not proof of a preferred explanation. Repetition does not transform an allegation into fact.

## Initial course and product sequence

### Course 1 — Security Without Omniscience

Learners design a threat-detection system that protects a fictional city without building unlimited surveillance.

Build projects:

- privacy-preserving event analysis
- role-based access control
- immutable audit logs
- data-retention limits
- human appeal and correction workflows

### Course 2 — The Stolen Name

Learners model financial, digital, medical, and AI identity theft and build a recovery navigator.

Build projects:

- identity incident ledger
- credential rotation workflow
- synthetic-media verification
- consent and revocation registry
- privacy-safe evidence manifest

### Course 3 — The War for Reality

Learners compare authentic records, selective leaks, fabricated documents, testimony, and AI-generated media.

Build projects:

- claim-to-source matrix
- media provenance verifier
- contradiction tracker
- source confidence scoring
- correction and supersession system

### Course 4 — The Disclosure Threshold

Learners examine UAP records through scientific and archival methods without treating uncertainty as proof.

Build projects:

- sensor provenance schema
- chain-of-custody ledger
- independent replication checklist
- witness testimony matrix
- official-assessment versus hypothesis dashboard

## Build Mode coding standards

- Use synthetic or public-domain datasets.
- Never use real stolen identity data or private health records.
- Do not create tools for unauthorized surveillance, intrusion, evasion, doxxing, or retaliation.
- Include tests, threat models, privacy impact notes, and limitations.
- Teach defensive and accountable systems rather than unrestricted monitoring.
- Require human approval for legal, medical, or named-person conclusions.
