# P-011-CRM-001 — CRM Educational Entertainment and Learning-to-Earning System

**Canonical source:** `lippytm/Prompt-11-`  
**Status:** Q2 educational mirror

## Purpose

Teach people, AI systems, robots, creators, programmers, blockchain developers, and entrepreneurs how to build ethical, testable relationship and customer-success systems through the Encyclopedia of Everything Applied and Fable 5 Educational Entertainment.

## Learning domains

- CRM parties, roles, relationships, and organizations;
- identity resolution and duplicate review;
- consent, suppression, retention, deletion, and privacy rights;
- customer and learner journeys;
- support cases, complaints, refunds, corrections, and service levels;
- data quality, provenance, timeliness, and audits;
- APIs, JSON schemas, Python, SQLite, tests, migrations, backup, and restore;
- HubSpot, Airtable, Slack, ManyChat, Zo, social, and future provider adapters;
- campaign, referral, affiliate, and revenue attribution;
- accessibility, fairness, cybersecurity, and environmental impact;
- AI Clone, Fabric Hermes, Fable 5, and CRM swarm passports;
- Quality Evidence Packets and human release decisions.

## Standard episode-to-build pattern

1. Fictional character and relationship problem
2. Reality lesson and sources
3. IF, MAYBE, WHY NOT, and DON’T DO THAT questions
4. Data model and system diagram
5. Programming or blockchain build
6. Tests, diagnostics, and debugging
7. Privacy, consent, accessibility, fairness, and security review
8. Customer or learner success application
9. Entrepreneurship and transparent value hypothesis
10. Proof of learning
11. Next mutation
12. Correction, retirement, and archive

## Initial Fable 5 CRM story arc

1. **Lippy Killjoy and the Customer Who Existed Twice** — duplicate resolution and identity safety.
2. **The Consent Clock That Would Not Stop** — consent, suppression, retention, and deletion.
3. **The Support Ticket at the End of Eternity** — service levels, escalation, and correction.
4. **The Attribution Machine That Claimed Every Sale** — campaign attribution and accounting boundaries.
5. **The Clone Who Sent the Message Too Soon** — AI disclosure, communication approval, and HumanApprovalGate.

## Learning-to-Earning boundary

Approved learners may earn through verified contributions, documentation, testing, accessibility work, educational media, support, or certified project delivery. The program does not guarantee income, employment, funding, investment returns, certification, or business success.

## Fiction boundary

Fable 5, Lippy Killjoy, Nexus Nine, and other extraordinary characters remain fictional dramatizations. Fictional identities must never be merged with real-person CRM records.

## Quality requirements

Each learning product needs measurable objectives, prerequisites, worked examples, learner exercises, tests, debugging guidance, accessibility adaptations, source and rights review, privacy and security review, outcome evidence, corrections, and human approval.

## Current boundary

This Q2 mirror defines the curriculum and product architecture. Finished commercial courses, ebooks, audiobooks, videos, credentials, and franchise programs require separate Q3/Q4 evidence and approval.

## Slack CRM Integration

The AI Slack CRM Systems integration extends this learning system into Slack as the primary learner interaction surface. Key connection points:

- **Party Passport** maps to `LearnerProfile` in the Slack CRM database (PostgreSQL)
- **Learner journey** tracked via `LearningInteraction` records, synced to Fabric every 5 minutes
- **Support cases** use AI-triage (GPT-4o classification) matching the support SLA monitor Build Mode project
- **Provider adapter** for Slack is implemented via Slack Bolt for Python with Socket Mode
- **On-chain credential issuance** fulfills the blockchain provenance proof Build Mode project
