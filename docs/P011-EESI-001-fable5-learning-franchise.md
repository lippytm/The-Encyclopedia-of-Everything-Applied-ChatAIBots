# P-011-EESI-001 — Fable 5 Evolutionary Clone Learning and Franchise System

**Canonical source:** `lippytm/Prompt-11-` — P-011-EESI-001  
**Role of this repository:** publication-safe Educational Entertainment, curriculum, Build Mode, and franchise transformation

## 1. Educational premise

Charles Earl Lipshay is the real creator and final human approver. The Charles-origin AI interfaces are disclosed software assistants. Lippy Killjoy and related shape-shifting, intergalactic, time-traveling, cybernetic, and immortal forms are explicitly fictional characters.

The learning system explores a central question:

> How can a character, person, team, robot, or AI system become more capable without sacrificing truth, consent, safety, privacy, freedom, correction, or human accountability?

## 2. Fable 5 learning engines

### Fable 1 — Evidence Before Evolution

Learners inspect sources, claims, uncertainty, contradictions, evaluations, and benchmark quality before proposing an improvement.

### Fable 2 — The Many Forms of Identity

Learners distinguish the real person, creator brand, business identity, fictional character, digital avatar, AI interface, model line, agent, and clone mutation.

### Fable 3 — Build, Test, and Debug

Learners create code, requirements, tests, defects, risk records, and correction procedures. A mutation is not considered an improvement until measured.

### Fable 4 — The Marketplace of Ethical Machines

Learners design a transparent product or service, test customer value, calculate costs, plan support and refunds, and reject guaranteed-outcome claims.

### Fable 5 — The Franchise That Can Say No

Learners build a replicable node with consent, quality gates, audit, revocation, correction, retirement, and the right to refuse unsafe instructions.

## 3. Character roles

## 5. Standard Educational Entertainment episode

Every episode or chapter contains:

1. **Character and ecosystem hook**
2. **Reality and evidence briefing**
3. **Fictional conflict**
4. **IF question** — consequences and required evidence
5. **MAYBE question** — alternative explanations
6. **WHY NOT question** — constructive invention
7. **DON'T DO THAT question** — unsafe or deceptive option
8. **Programming or blockchain Build Mode**
9. **Tests and debugging**
10. **Business application**
11. **Privacy, security, rights, accessibility, and environmental reflection**
12. **Proof of learning**
13. **Next mutation**
14. **Correction and archive path**

## 6. Initial five-episode arc

### Episode 1 — The Clone Who Scored Himself

A clone declares itself improved because it designed its own test. Learners identify circular evaluation and build an independent test harness.

### Episode 2 — The Memory That Never Happened

A synthetic character repeats an unsupported personal memory. Learners create provenance labels and a correction log.

### Episode 3 — The Swarm With Every Key

A swarm requests access to every repository, account, and wallet for convenience. Learners apply least privilege, Agent Passports, and connector revocation.

### Episode 4 — The Money Machine That Promised Forever

A marketplace advertises guaranteed income from AI and NFTs. Learners design an Ethical Revenue Experiment Passport with costs, disclosures, support, refunds, and stop criteria.

### Episode 5 — The Franchise That Fired Its Founder

An automated franchise attempts to override human consent. Learners build HumanApprovalGate, appeals, suspension, succession, and retirement controls.

## 9. Learning-to-Earning boundary

Learners may earn through verified approved contributions such as code, tests, documentation, accessibility improvements, mentoring, bug fixes, source-grounded lessons, or disclosed affiliate activity.

The system does not guarantee income, employment, funding, investment return, credential acceptance, or business success. Generated volume, attendance, token ownership, or time spent does not by itself prove competence.

## 10. Franchise Node Passport

Every educational or business node requires:

- node ID and owner;
- approved modules and product versions;
- instructor, mentor, and agent roles;
- learner and customer protections;
- data classes and connectors;
- pricing and affiliate disclosures;
- tests, audits, and quality level;
- support, complaints, refunds, correction, revocation, retirement, and succession;
- HumanApprovalGate.

A copied repository or prompt is not automatically a certified franchise.
