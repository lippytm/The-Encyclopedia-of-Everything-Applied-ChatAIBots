## Pilot story

**Lippy Killjoy and the Quality Machine That Graded Itself**

A Business of Businesses installs an AI quality machine that marks every system “perfect” because it is allowed to define its own test, pass its own gates, erase its defects, and approve its own release. Lippy Killjoy and the Fable 5 crew rebuild it with independent evidence, consent, provider passports, red-team tests, correction, rollback, and human approval.

## Learning sequence

1. Map Business Ecosystems and relationship edges.
2. Separate real identity, business identity, disclosed AI interface, and fictional character.
3. Model CRM consent, journeys, support, learning, quality, providers, and corrections.
4. Build a Python or JavaScript telemetry validator.
5. Store missions and evolution proposals in SQLite or another approved database.
6. Add JSON Schemas and automated tests.
7. Add blockchain-style hashes and supersession records without putting private CRM data on-chain.
8. Test accessibility, fairness, privacy, security, environmental impact, and human override.
9. Design a no-transaction entrepreneurship experiment.
10. Produce a Quality Evidence Packet and release decision.

Learners may earn recognition or compensation for verified approved contribution. The program does not guarantee income, employment, funding, certification, investment returns, or business success.

Extraordinary character abilities and cosmic systems remain Fictional Dramatization. Real CRM, privacy, security, programming, blockchain, business, and quality claims require sources, tests, uncertainty, and correction.
