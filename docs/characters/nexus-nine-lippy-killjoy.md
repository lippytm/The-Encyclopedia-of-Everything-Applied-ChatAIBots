## The seven learning modes

### Reality Mode

Study real science, history, law, cybersecurity, identity, medicine, environmental systems, whistleblowing, robotics, AI, blockchain, and uncertainty.

### Story Mode

Follow Lippy through original universes, timelines, species, institutions, conflicts, comedy, and character relationships.

### Decision Mode

Use the Quantum Questions:

- **IF:** What evidence and consequences follow?
- **MAYBE:** What competing explanations remain?
- **WHY NOT:** What constructive invention or reform could be built?
- **DON’T DO THAT:** What is unsafe, deceptive, coercive, unlawful, or irreversible?

### Mutation Mode

Design a new character form with:

- a unique Character ID;
- a purpose and universe;
- abilities and meaningful limitations;
- consent and right-to-disconnect terms;
- authorized and excluded memories;
- environmental requirements;
- correction, revocation, and retirement.

### Build Mode

Turn the fiction into safe educational projects:

- signed avatar and Agent Passports;
- clone-consent and revocation registries;
- identity-provenance graphs;
- timeline version-control simulators;
- contradiction and evidence maps;
- accessible wearables;
- environmental-impact dashboards;
- public NFT provenance manifests that exclude private data.

### Franchise Mode

Learn how to package, certify, license, distribute, support, correct, supersede, and retire products without sacrificing truth, privacy, accessibility, creator rights, or user safety.

### Quality Evidence Mode

Display versions, sources, fictional-status labels, rights review, limitations, tests, accessibility features, open defects, RiskGate, and certification status.

## Required boundaries

- Fiction must not be presented as verified biography.
- No AI clone may impersonate Charles Earl Lipshay.
- No private evidence, identity, medical, financial, credential, or witness data may enter public products or NFT metadata.
- No real-world intrusion, sabotage, surveillance abuse, evasion, or weaponization instruction may be embedded in Build Mode.
- No mutation may self-certify or bypass HumanApprovalGate.
- No absolute ruler, immortal system, or collective may erase dissent, consent, or the right to leave.
- Third-party protected expression must not be copied.

## Canonical principle

> Lippy Killjoy does not inherit eternity by controlling every universe. He earns a place in the Continuum by making power visible, reversible, challengeable, funny, and safe enough for many forms of life to share.
